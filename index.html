Lónyay utca. 48 hours.

The run ends Sunday 08:00, in 30 real minutes. Only money from the machines counts.

Fill your bag from streets, planters, bins, party leftovers and courtyards, then feed it to the bottle machine at SPAR (S) or Aldi in the market hall (A). Open 06:00–24:00. Items in the bag are worth nothing until they go through a machine. Each bag holds 50; find up to two more. Eat at the till before you starve, mind the cars, and go easy on the beer.
